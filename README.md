# Salon Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Business Problem
The salon needed a clear way to monitor revenue, expenses, customer retention, and service performance without relying on manual spreadsheet reporting.

## Solution
Built a business intelligence dashboard using anonymized sample salon data. The dashboard tracks operational and financial KPIs to help management understand performance and identify improvement opportunities.

## Tools Used
- Power BI-style dashboard design
- SQL-ready relational dataset
- Excel/CSV sample data
- Python for sample data generation and visualization
- Business KPI analysis

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Average Ticket Size
- Active Customers
- Returning Customer Rate
- Revenue by Service
- Expenses by Category
- Monthly Revenue vs Profit

## Business Impact
- Centralized business reporting into one dashboard
- Reduced manual reporting dependency
- Improved visibility into revenue, expenses, and service performance
- Supported better operational decision-making
